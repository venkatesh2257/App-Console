Organization Master Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Bavya Health Services Private Limited (BHSPL)
Effective Date: January 10, 2025  |  Last Revised & Updated: September 22, 2026

This Master Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, digital platforms, and software services developed, owned, or operated by Bavya Health Services Private Limited (BHSPL) ("we", "our", "us", or "Company").

Whether you are an operational staff member, healthcare worker, emergency response driver, field supervisor, or a citizen user, this policy explains how your information is collected, processed, and safeguarded across all applications published under the Bavya Health Services ecosystem.

1. Scope of Covered Applications

This single consolidated policy governs the following categories of mobile applications published by BHSPL on the Google Play Store and enterprise distribution channels:

🚑 Field Operations & Emergency Workforce

Covered Apps: BHSPL BTGS, BHSPL Ops 108, Fleet & Asset Checklist Apps.
Package Identifiers: com.bavya.btgs, in.bhspl.ops108
Audience: Authorized on-duty healthcare workers, ambulance crews, field inspection staff.

🏥 Citizen Health & Public Services

Covered Apps: 104 Citizen Health App, Public Care & Information Portals.
Package Identifiers: in.bhspl.citizen104 (and related citizen portals)
Audience: General public seeking healthcare advisory, telemedicine, or emergency assistance.

2. Information We Collect

The type of data collected depends upon whether you use an operational workforce application or a citizen service application:

A. Operational & Workforce Applications (e.g., BHSPL BTGS / Ops 108)

  • Staff Account Credentials: Full Name, Employee ID / Staff Code, Official Mobile Number, Assigned Role, and Base Station/District for authenticated duty login.
  • Shift & Inspection Records: Daily duty logs, ambulance checklist submissions, vehicle odometer/fuel readings, medical equipment readiness status, and digital sign-offs.
  • Diagnostic & Device Telemetry: Device hardware model, operating system version, app performance logs, and network connectivity state.

B. Citizen & Public Health Applications (e.g., 104 Citizen App)

  • Basic Profile Details: User name, contact phone number, and city/district (provided voluntarily during registration).
  • Health Service Requests: Information submitted to schedule medical consultations, request tele-triage assistance, or locate nearby healthcare facilities.

3. Device Permissions & Usage Purpose Matrix

Our applications strictly adhere to data minimization. We only request device permissions that are directly necessary to perform core features:

Permission / API Applicable App Category Operational Purpose Collection Trigger

Prominent Disclosure on Location Services:
For workforce applications (e.g., com.bavya.btgs), location data is accessed in the foreground and background strictly to coordinate emergency response services and confirm official duty presence. Location logs are stored on secure enterprise servers, are accessible only by authorized dispatchers, and are never shared or used for commercial advertising.
Official App Store Distribution Policy:
All BHSPL mobile applications are published and updated exclusively through official distribution channels (such as the Google Play Store). Our apps do not request, download, or install external APK packages outside official app store mechanisms.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

We implement enterprise-grade security controls to protect user data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Data Encryption in Transit: All communications between our mobile apps and central cloud servers (ops.bhspl.in) are protected using industry-standard SSL/TLS (Transport Layer Security) encryption.
  • Strict Confidentiality (No Data Sale): We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade user personal data or location coordinates with third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Access to sensitive operational records is restricted strictly to authenticated administrative officers and supervisors.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain user data only for as long as necessary to fulfill operational duties, administrative governance, and statutory audit compliance.

How to Request Account & Personal Data Deletion:

Users of any Bavya Health Services application can submit an account and data deletion request at any time through either of the following methods:

  • Online Deletion Request Portal: Submit your request directly on our web portal at https://ops.bhspl.in/account-deletion.
  • Email Request: Send an email to our Privacy Compliance Officer at qualitycontrol@bhspl.in with your registered Employee ID / Phone Number and application name.

Upon verification of your identity, your personal profile data and login credentials will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 30 days, subject to statutory healthcare records and audit retention regulations.

6. Children's Privacy

Our workforce and operational management applications are intended strictly for adult professional personnel. Our citizen health applications do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 years of age without parental or guardian consent. If we learn that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected without verified consent, we will delete that data immediately.

7. Changes to This Master Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Master Privacy Policy to incorporate new application releases, feature enhancements, or evolving regulatory standards. When changes occur, the revised date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
